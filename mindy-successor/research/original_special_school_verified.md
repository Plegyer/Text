# Simply Mindy — verified special school / stat-swap scene

Источник: SceneEvents_251 (schoolGypsy) + SceneEvents_0 entry handler.

## Entry

The eighth school option is a separate scene, schoolGypsy (scene 251).

Main checks Stamina > 0 before switching to it and then subtracts exactly 1 Stamina immediately after the scene switch.

So the guide is correct that this visit also costs one action.

## Two functions

Carla offers:

1. Swap Stats
2. Buy Titles

There is no tuition fee just for entering beyond the 1 Stamina action cost. Money is only needed when purchasing Titles.

## Stat swap

The UI explicitly states a 2:1 exchange ratio.

The player:
- chooses a stat to give up;
- chooses multiplier x1 / x2 / x3;
- chooses a different stat to receive;
- confirms the trade.

Verified quantities:
- x1: lose 2, gain 1
- x2: lose 4, gain 2
- x3: lose 6, gain 3

The source stat must contain at least 2/4/6 respectively.

All eleven normal stats are available as source and destination:
Charisma, Strength, Durability, Prowess, Intellect, Spirit, Fuckability, Cleanliness, Creativity, Sociability, Class.

This makes the school system partly reversible: inefficient job gains can be converted into a needed gate stat at a 50% loss.

## Purchasable basic Titles

This scene sells six daily-stat titles:

- Yoo-Hoo: +1 Charisma after apartment sleep — $15,000
- Neat Freak: +1 Cleanliness — $15,000
- Tits For Days: +1 Fuckability — $20,000
- Gossip Girl: +1 Sociability — $15,000
- Life of the Party: +1 Class — $15,000
- Artiste: +1 Creativity — $15,000

The remaining Titles are primarily unlocked through endings/other systems.

## Architectural role

The eighth school action is not another stat package. It is a late-economy correction/customization service:

- convert surplus stats into missing stats;
- spend large amounts of cash on persistent Title unlocks;
- still consume one daily action.

This gives excess money and imperfect builds a recovery valve without removing the cost of specialization.
