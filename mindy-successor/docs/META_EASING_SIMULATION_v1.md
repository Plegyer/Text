## 1. Fresh theoretical minima

Normal lodging, 3 Energy/day, specialized sprint:

- Tavern day 10
- Guard day 13
- Merchant day 21
- Hunter day 22
- Prostitute day 31
- Assassin day 32
- Artist/Geisha day 33

## 2. After all three starter Hard Work endings

Automatic rewards:

- Lumber: +3 Strength/+3 Endurance
- Satyr Maid: +3 Charm/+3 Grooming
- Farm: +3 Reason/+3 Magic

Same fresh economy.

Theoretical minima move only slightly:
- Tavern day 9
- Guard roughly unchanged in current optimizer because Manners remains the bottleneck
- Hunter roughly unchanged because Wits/Dexterity remain limiting
- Merchant roughly unchanged because Wits remains limiting
- Artist about one day earlier

This is acceptable:
three simple endings help builds, but do not yet destroy early survival.

## 3. After starter + four mid Hard Work endings

Additional:
- Tavern +3 Sociability/+3 Manners
- Hunter +3 Dexterity/+3 Wits
- Guard +3 Endurance/+3 Manners
- Merchant +600 starting money

Candidate starting money becomes 1200 instead of 600.

Late-job theoretical minima:

- Prostitute: ~day 22
- Assassin: ~day 26
- Artist/Geisha: ~day 30

Fresh:
- 31 / 32 / 33.

So simple labor meta alone already removes roughly one to ten days depending on gate.

## 4. Why bigger easing still comes from complex endings

Hard Work is only one ending family.

The intended large difficulty collapse comes when the player also earns complex rewards such as:
- +Energy;
- rent relief;
- daily income;
- stronger carry-over.

The earlier illustrative mid-meta fixture showed:
- mid jobs around day 4–6;
- late jobs around day 13–24.

This is desirable.

## 5. Full 10/10 profile

All Hard Work rewards provide:
- every progression stat at least +3 above fresh baseline, directly or through stacked rewards;
- several stats +6;
- +600 starting money;
- plus 10 free creation points.

Even without complex perks, early School requirements are much smaller.

With complex perks, ordinary job gates can become almost trivial.

That is not a balance failure; it is the intended NG+ arc.

## 6. Conclusion

Meta progression should be tested by asking:

**How much earlier can the player reach chosen content?**

not:

**How do we preserve fresh difficulty forever?**

The current Hard Work reward ladder passes that qualitative test.

Exact values remain tunable.
