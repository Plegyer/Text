## 1. Core purpose

School одновременно:

1. **конвертирует деньги + Energy в нужные stats**;
2. исправляет build после seasonal work;
3. помогает добивать career gates;
4. содержит собственные teachers/milestones/projects/relationships.

Пункт 1 не должен быть принесён в жертву сюжетной глубине.

## 2. Universal accessibility rule

Каждый из 12 progression stats имеет repeatable paid School source.

Не обязательно 12 отдельных кнопок.

Один курс может давать крупный multi-stat package, как в оригинале.

## 4. Price/gain ladder

School should resemble the useful original pattern:

- cheap courses: smaller targeted package;
- middle courses: larger 2–3 stat package;
- expensive courses: strong multi-stat package.

So paying more can be more Energy-efficient.

Example shape only:

```text
cheap:
  cost ~ low
  +2 main +1 secondary

middle:
  cost ~ medium
  +2/+2 or +3/+2

advanced:
  cost ~ high
  +3/+3/+2
```

Exact values after economy simulation.

## 5. Special correction service

Target includes a late School/campus service analogous in function to original stat swap.

Requirement:
- choose any progression stat as source;
- choose any different progression stat as destination;
- convert at an inefficient ratio.

Default candidate:
- lose 2;
- gain 1.

This gives player a universal correction valve.

Could require:
- School milestone;
- money;
- special NPC;
- meta unlock.

But it must not depend on a rare random event.

## 6. Milestones still matter

Repeatable paid lessons increase stats immediately.

Track progress can additionally unlock:
- certificates;
- projects;
- teacher lines;
- career public facts;
- education endings.

Thus:
```text
lesson = reliable stats
milestone = systemic/story unlock
```

## 7. Night study

Night study remains:
- no normal tuition;
- useful stat/course progress;
- -1 next-day Energy.

It should be less efficient than paid advanced School for raw stats but useful when money is scarce.

## 8. Meta easing

Endings may improve School through:
- discounts;
- stronger lesson packages;
- starting certificates;
- free first lessons;
- stat carry-over;
- unlocked advanced course.

This is desirable because NG+ should become easier.

## 9. Acceptance

School succeeds when:

1. player can deliberately raise any progression stat;
2. work-money-school loop can repair any seasonal build;
3. all 12 stats have direct repeatable access;
4. expensive lessons are more Energy-efficient than cheap ones;
5. special conversion can transform surplus stat into missing stat;
6. career gate can be reached by working for money and studying even if current job does not raise that stat;
7. milestones add depth without blocking basic stat acquisition;
8. meta rewards can make School materially easier in later lives.
