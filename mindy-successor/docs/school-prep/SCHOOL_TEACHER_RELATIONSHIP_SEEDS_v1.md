## Relationship-boundary rules

All seeds are optional.

They may become:
- presentation variants;
- relationship-first-impression scenes;
- repeated-contact scenes;
- reactions to already-approved public results;
- career/world commentary.

They must not become:
- ordinary paid-course prerequisites;
- extra tuition or Energy charges;
- duplicate School progress;
- hidden milestone providers;
- unapproved public-result publishers.

## Save / reload

If a seed becomes a real relationship scene:
- School transaction commits first;
- pending relationship scene survives reload;
- resolving it does not replay the School transaction;
- relationship state changes once in the relationship domain;
- any durable cross-system trigger uses an approved public boundary.

## Night / social hooks

Night/social hooks remain distinct from School Night-study transactions.

A teacher social scene:
- does not increment `night_study_sessions`;
- does not apply the Night-study +2 stat effect;
- does not apply next-day Energy -1 unless the actual School Night-study action is performed;
- does not consume School tuition.
