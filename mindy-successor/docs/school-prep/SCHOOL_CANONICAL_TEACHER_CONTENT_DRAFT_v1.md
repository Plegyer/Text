## Track notes

### Literacy & Numbers — Mara Fen

Core fantasy:
- practical literacy;
- ledgers and everyday arithmetic;
- planning and clear written thought.

Voice:
- short examples;
- concrete questions;
- occasional understated jokes when a student overcomplicates a simple problem.

Relationship seed:
- remembers the player's earlier mistakes and improvements;
- may later trust the player with more meaningful civic/accounting examples.

### Arcana — Ilyra Venn

Core fantasy:
- disciplined observation;
- controlled magical reasoning;
- curiosity without recklessness.

Voice:
- asks what was actually observed before discussing theory;
- treats uncertainty as useful information.

Relationship seed:
- intellectual trust grows when the player admits uncertainty rather than bluffing.

### Rhetoric & Social — Cassian Reed

Core fantasy:
- persuasion;
- listening;
- reading a room;
- thinking while speaking.

Voice:
- conversational;
- fast drills;
- deliberate reframing of awkward answers into stronger ones.

Relationship seed:
- playful competitive exchanges can become professional respect or friendship.

### Etiquette & Presentation — Eliane Marrow

Core fantasy:
- social codes;
- grooming;
- posture;
- deliberate presentation.

Voice:
- economical;
- notices details;
- corrects without humiliating the student.

Relationship seed:
- respect grows from preparation and consistency rather than charm alone.

### Field Craft — Rowan Pike

Core fantasy:
- observation;
- dexterity;
- route sense;
- practical improvisation.

Voice:
- sparse instructions;
- questions about terrain, tools and consequences.

Relationship seed:
- trust emerges when the player proves dependable under small practical pressures.

### Physical Training — Tovan Kest

Core fantasy:
- strength;
- endurance;
- coordination;
- sustainable discipline.

Voice:
- direct;
- breaks difficult work into repeatable form;
- discourages reckless showing off.

Relationship seed:
- coach-like approval develops through consistency rather than exceptional single performances.

### Arts & Performance — Nessa Lark

Core fantasy:
- creativity;
- expression;
- timing;
- performance confidence.

Voice:
- vivid metaphors;
- asks what a choice is meant to communicate;
- welcomes experimentation but expects commitment.

Relationship seed:
- shared rehearsal work can support friendship or artistic rivalry.

### Body Confidence — Sera Vey

Core fantasy:
- grooming;
- physical self-awareness;
- deliberate presence;
- comfort in one's own presentation.

Voice:
- calm and matter-of-fact;
- focuses on posture, care, boundaries and self-command.

Relationship seed:
- trust may grow through honest conversations about confidence and presentation.
