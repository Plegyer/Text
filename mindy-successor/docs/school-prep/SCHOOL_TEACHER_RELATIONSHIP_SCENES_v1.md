# School Teacher Relationship Scenes v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT. Optional teacher relationship-scene material for the eight drafted School teachers.

These scenes do not gate paid lessons, alter School transactions, or create canonical relationship routes. Any durable relationship state must be authored and owned by the relationship domain.

## Mara Fen — Literacy & Numbers

### Repeated contact — The Better Explanation
Mara remembers that you once overcomplicated a simple calculation. This time she asks you to explain the same kind of problem first. When you choose a cleaner explanation, she notices immediately.

Potential use:
- repeated-contact presentation;
- mentor/friendship seed;
- no School prerequisite.

### Trust moment — The Unfinished Ledger
Mara shows you a deliberately incomplete civic ledger and asks what information you would need before making a recommendation. She seems more interested in whether you admit the limits of the evidence than in whether you guess correctly.

### Friction moment — Too Clever
You find a shortcut that works mathematically but would confuse anyone else reading the record. Mara objects to the method, not the result, and the disagreement becomes about responsibility rather than intelligence.

### Night/social invitation shape
Mara may invite you to help sort a stack of public notices or records after class. The scene is social/presentation content only unless a separate relationship route is approved.

### Career/world reaction
Mara may comment on an approved management/accounting public result, civic work, or merchant outcome without reading School-private counters.

## Ilyra Venn — Arcana

### Repeated contact — The Question You Kept
Ilyra recalls a question you left unanswered in an earlier lesson and asks whether you still think it matters. She respects the fact that you kept it rather than forcing a conclusion.

### Trust moment — Admit Uncertainty
A dangerous-looking observation has several possible explanations. Ilyra relaxes slightly when you say what you do not know before offering a theory.

### Friction moment — Elegant Guess
You propose an explanation that is clever and unsupported. Ilyra is colder than usual: “Beauty is not evidence.” The tension can become intellectual rivalry or deeper respect.

### Night/social invitation shape
Ilyra may invite you to review sealed diagrams or compare observation notes after the practical room is closed. No extra Arcana mechanics occur.

### Career/world reaction
Ilyra may react to approved magical-anomaly, Forest, research, Palace, or knowledge public outcomes.

## Cassian Reed — Rhetoric & Social

### Repeated contact — The Callback
Cassian reuses a phrase you once stumbled over and turns it into a joke, then asks whether you want another attempt. The teasing is light enough to decline.

### Trust moment — Say What You Actually Want
After a public exercise, Cassian asks privately whether your stated goal was the real one. The useful part of the scene is the permission to answer honestly.

### Friction moment — Winning the Room
You win a debate by embarrassing the other student. Cassian acknowledges the technical success but questions whether you achieved the result you actually needed.

### Night/social invitation shape
Cassian may invite you to an informal debate, public gathering, or conversation debrief. The invitation does not consume School Night-study mechanics.

### Career/world reaction
Cassian may comment on approved social, Guard, Merchant, Tavern, Palace, or public-speaking outcomes.

## Eliane Marrow — Etiquette & Presentation

### Repeated contact — The Detail She Stops Correcting
Eliane notices a habit she used to correct and says nothing about it. Only later does she mention that she stopped because you had already fixed it.

### Trust moment — Private Correction
Eliane gives you a discreet warning before a formal situation rather than correcting you afterward in public. The gesture signals growing professional trust.

### Friction moment — Rule Without Purpose
You challenge a formal convention that seems pointless. Eliane insists that you first understand what it was meant to protect before deciding whether to ignore it.

### Night/social invitation shape
Eliane may invite you to prepare for or debrief a formal event. The scene can support presentation or relationship content without changing paid-course access.

### Career/world reaction
Eliane may react to approved Palace, Artist, Merchant, Guard, or status-related public outcomes.

## Rowan Pike — Field Craft

### Repeated contact — You Packed It This Time
Rowan notices that you remembered a small tool or precaution he once criticized you for forgetting. He marks the improvement with a short nod and moves on.

### Trust moment — Take the Lead
For a small, low-risk field problem, Rowan lets you choose the route and follows your plan without correcting every decision.

### Friction moment — Unnecessary Risk
You choose the faster route when the safer one was clearly adequate. Rowan’s frustration is practical, not moral: “If you spend risk for no reason, you will eventually need it when you have none left.”

### Night/social invitation shape
Rowan may ask you to review maps, equipment, or tomorrow’s route over a quiet meal or camp-style scene.

### Career/world reaction
Rowan may comment on approved Hunter, Forest, travel, field, or survival public outcomes.

## Tovan Kest — Physical Training

### Repeated contact — Consistency
Tovan points out that your form is improving because you kept showing up rather than because one session went exceptionally well.

### Trust moment — Call the Limit
He asks you to stop a drill when you decide your form is deteriorating. When you do, he treats the judgment as progress rather than weakness.

### Friction moment — Show-Off Set
You push far past the agreed pace to prove a point. Tovan ends the drill and makes the disagreement about reliability, not toughness.

### Night/social invitation shape
Tovan may invite you on a recovery walk, equipment check, or quiet coaching conversation. It remains distinct from School Night study.

### Career/world reaction
Tovan may react to approved Guard, caravan, physical-career, injury-recovery, or work-discipline outcomes.

## Nessa Lark — Arts & Performance

### Repeated contact — The Choice She Remembers
Nessa remembers an unusual artistic choice from an earlier rehearsal and asks whether you still believe in it. The callback creates continuity without requiring a progression gate.

### Trust moment — Show the Unfinished Version
Nessa shares an unfinished performance idea and asks for a reaction before she has polished it enough to feel safe.

### Friction moment — Imitation
Your work leans too heavily on another performer’s mannerisms. Nessa challenges you to decide whether you are learning from the source or hiding inside it.

### Night/social invitation shape
Nessa may invite you to a rehearsal, performance, gallery, music session, or collaborative idea exchange.

### Career/world reaction
Nessa may react to approved Artist, Tavern, Palace, festival, performance, or public-cultural outcomes.

## Sera Vey — Body Confidence

### Repeated contact — Deliberate Choice
Sera notices that a presentation choice you once made nervously now looks intentional. She asks what changed in how you decided.

### Trust moment — A Boundary Respected
You decline a suggestion and explain why it does not suit you. Sera accepts the answer without trying to persuade you, reinforcing that the lesson is about agency rather than conformity.

### Friction moment — Performing for Approval
Sera challenges a choice that appears designed entirely around anticipated judgment. The disagreement is about ownership of the choice, not whether the choice itself is acceptable.

### Night/social invitation shape
Sera may invite you to a quiet preparation, grooming, movement, or social debrief scene outside class.

### Career/world reaction
Sera may react to approved social, service, Artist, Palace, status, or self-presentation outcomes.

## Relationship-route boundary

These scenes may later become:
- first-impression callbacks;
- mentor/friendship content;
- professional respect;
- rivalry/friction;
- optional social invitations;
- reactions to approved public results.

They do not decide:
- canonical relationship IDs;
- romance scope;
- relationship thresholds;
- relationship stage names;
- ending eligibility;
- Meta rewards.

## Paid lesson independence

No scene in this bank may:
- be required to enroll in a paid lesson;
- charge tuition;
- spend School Energy;
- change the course stat package;
- increment `lessons_completed`;
- change Night-study counters;
- replace the ordinary School picker.

## Night separation

A teacher social/Night invitation:
- is not the School Night-study action;
- does not add +2 to a supported stat;
- does not apply next-day Energy -1;
- does not increment `night_study_sessions`;
- may consume Night only if a separately authored Night event/relationship rule explicitly does so.

## Cross-system reactions

Career/world reactions use:
- approved public School results;
- approved public career/world state;
- ordinary typed stats where the design explicitly allows them.

They do not read School-private counters, certificates, projects, exams, or milestone state directly.
