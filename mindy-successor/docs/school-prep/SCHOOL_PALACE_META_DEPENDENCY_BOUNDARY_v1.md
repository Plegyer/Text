# School Palace / Meta Dependency Boundary v1

Status: ownership and dependency boundary for School contributions to Palace and Meta progression.

## Palace unlock remains independent of School

Locked Palace unlock rule:

- Palace unlock depends on **Hard Work on all 10 ordinary careers only**.
- School is not an additional Palace unlock prerequisite.
- School lesson count is not part of the unlock.
- School certificates are not part of the unlock.
- School public results are not part of the unlock.
- Teacher relationships are not part of the unlock.
- Arcana/management results are not part of the unlock.

Changing this rule requires a new direct Product decision. It must not happen through School authoring by implication.

## What School may influence after Palace unlock

After the independent Palace unlock is already satisfied, authored School results may affect Palace content.

Draft-compatible School contributions include:
- literacy/accounting public result;
- etiquette public result;
- rhetoric public result;
- Arcana/knowledge public result;
- approved School certificate;
- approved education-history/ending result.

Possible Palace-owned effects:
- alternate dialogue;
- archive access;
- ritual/knowledge interpretation;
- administrative option;
- ceremonial/social option;
- route-specific solution;
- presentation variant.

These are Palace content options, not Palace unlock requirements.

## Ownership boundary

School owns:
- School-private progression;
- milestones;
- certificates;
- projects/exams;
- approved public-result publication.

Palace owns:
- Palace stages;
- Palace options;
- Palace relationships;
- Palace routes/outcomes;
- Palace ending eligibility.

Boundary:

`School private result -> approved durable public/Meta result -> Palace-owned option`.

Palace must not read:
- `lessons_completed`;
- `night_study_sessions`;
- private School milestone arrays;
- private School certificate arrays.

School must not directly advance a Palace private stage.

## Education endings

Education-specific endings, if retained, remain separate from ordinary-career Hard Work counting.

Still OPEN:
- ending IDs/names;
- School tracks involved;
- milestone/certificate/project/exam requirements;
- thresholds;
- teacher involvement;
- ending rewards;
- Meta effects.

An education ending may create an approved Meta result, but that result does not retroactively alter how Palace unlock is earned unless Product explicitly changes Palace unlock policy.

## Meta rewards

School may contribute Meta rewards only through explicit authored outcomes.

For every approved Meta reward:
- source School ending/result is explicit;
- canonical Meta reward ID is explicit;
- reward applies exactly once;
- reload does not reapply reward;
- New Run preserves only the approved Meta state;
- in-run School private counters are not copied into a new life unless a separate rule explicitly says so.

Meta reward details remain OPEN.

## Palace content after unlock

If Product authors School-sensitive Palace content, every option must record:
- consuming Palace event/option ID;
- approved School public/Meta result;
- player-facing reason;
- Palace-owned effect;
- save/reload behavior;
- presentation ID;
- whether the option is repeatable.

No option may infer an unauthored School certificate from stats alone.

## Hard Work separation

Ordinary-career Hard Work and School are distinct systems.

School must not:
- add an 11th Hard Work requirement;
- replace one of the 10 ordinary-career Hard Work requirements;
- grant ordinary-career Hard Work credit;
- waive a missing ordinary-career Hard Work requirement;
- make Palace unlock depend on management/Arcana/teacher state.

School may still make post-unlock Palace play richer.

## Relationship boundary

A teacher/presenter relationship cannot grant Palace unlock.

A teacher relationship may contribute to Palace content only when Product explicitly authors a public/Meta bridge.

Palace relationship state remains Palace-owned.

## Save / reload

Verify any future School -> Palace/Meta hook:
- School result publishes once;
- Palace option remains equivalently available after reload;
- resolving Palace scene does not republish School result;
- Meta reward applies once;
- New Run retains approved Meta reward;
- Palace unlock state still derives from the 10 ordinary-career Hard Work requirements only.

## Product decisions still OPEN

- exact School public results used by Palace;
- exact certificates, if any;
- exact Palace options/listeners;
- education-specific endings;
- education Meta reward IDs/effects;
- teacher/relationship contribution to Palace content;
- final presentation assets.

No exact Palace/School content or reward is selected by this boundary document.
