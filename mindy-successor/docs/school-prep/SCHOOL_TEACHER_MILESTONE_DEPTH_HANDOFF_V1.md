## Scope

This content adds depth around the two existing School paths relevant to the current Lumber handoff:
- Literacy & Numbers with `school.teacher.mara-fen`;
- Arcana with `school.teacher.ilyra-venn`.

No new teacher, course, milestone ID, threshold, certificate, world fact, or mechanics effect is created.

## Mara Fen depth

Prepared RU/EN variants focus on:
- visible improvement in record structure rather than raw speed;
- correcting an error through an auditable process;
- a handoff exercise where another student can use the record without the author present.

These scenes make the School line about responsibility, clarity, and transferable work rather than stat gain alone.

## Ilyra Venn depth

Prepared RU/EN variants focus on:
- recognizing uncertainty while preserving a path to an answer;
- valuing reproducible method when the conclusion is wrong;
- independently challenging an overly persuasive observation without teacher prompting.

These scenes make Arcana about learned reasoning habits rather than magic gain alone.

## Milestone neutrality

Every teacher has three attachment roles:
- `pre_milestone_depth`;
- `milestone_neutral_feedback`;
- `post_result_reflection`.

The fixture intentionally leaves the actual milestone IDs null. The roles are content-placement hints, not progression states.

The fixture also declares:
- no effects;
- no selected thresholds;
- no published world facts.

Integration may attach these variants only after an approved existing School boundary is selected.
