## Literacy & Numbers

Supported stats:
- reason;
- wits.

Quiet study:
You work through a short set of figures and notes by lamplight, checking each line twice before moving on.

Difficult session:
The numbers refuse to settle into a useful pattern. You slow down, recopy the problem, and keep working until the mistake becomes visible.

Successful insight:
A confusing passage suddenly becomes simpler when you rewrite it in your own words and reduce the problem to one clear question.

Fatigue context:
The work is slower than it would be during the day, but the quiet helps you focus on one idea at a time.

Cheap-housing flavor:
The room is cramped and the light is poor, so you keep the study materials close and work in short, careful bursts.

## Arcana

Supported stats:
- magic;
- reason;
- creativity.

Quiet study:
You review diagrams and observations without attempting anything dramatic, noting where theory and evidence still disagree.

Difficult session:
A sequence of symbols keeps slipping out of order. You stop trying to memorize it and reconstruct the logic from the beginning.

Successful insight:
A relationship between two notes becomes clear enough to explain without guesswork. You mark the connection and leave the rest for daylight.

Fatigue context:
Your concentration is thin, so you keep the work deliberately small: one diagram, one question, one clean conclusion.

Cheap-housing flavor:
With limited space, you avoid any practical experiment and use the night for reading, comparison, and careful written reasoning.

## Rhetoric & Social

Supported stats:
- charm;
- sociability;
- wits.

Quiet study:
You rehearse several ways to begin the same conversation and listen for how each opening changes the tone.

Difficult session:
Every version sounds forced. You stop trying to sound impressive and focus instead on what the other person would need to hear first.

Successful insight:
One awkward sentence becomes much easier when you change the goal from winning the exchange to keeping the conversation open.

Fatigue context:
You practice slowly, leaving longer pauses than usual and paying attention to what those silences communicate.

Cheap-housing flavor:
You keep your voice low and rehearse internally, shaping phrasing and timing without disturbing anyone nearby.

## Etiquette & Presentation

Supported stats:
- manners;
- grooming;
- charm.

Quiet study:
You rehearse introductions, posture, and preparation until each step feels deliberate rather than performative.

Difficult session:
A formal sequence becomes stiff when you overthink it. You strip it back to the purpose behind the rule and start again.

Successful insight:
One small adjustment makes the entire presentation feel more natural. The lesson is not to correct everything at once.

Fatigue context:
You keep the practice simple: prepare, check, breathe, and repeat without rushing the final result.

Cheap-housing flavor:
Limited space turns the session into careful preparation rather than movement-heavy practice: clothing, notes, posture, and timing.

## Field Craft

Supported stats:
- dexterity;
- wits;
- creativity.

Quiet study:
You sort route notes, knots, tool layouts, and sketches until tomorrow's choices become easier to picture.

Difficult session:
A knot or route plan keeps failing in the same place. You stop forcing it and rebuild the sequence from the last reliable step.

Successful insight:
A more efficient arrangement of tools reveals a solution that was hidden by clutter rather than complexity.

Fatigue context:
You practice only the safest small motions and spend the rest of the session reviewing what would matter in poor light or bad weather.

Cheap-housing flavor:
There is little room to spread out, so you organize the kit carefully and work with one tool or sketch at a time.

## Physical Training

Supported stats:
- strength;
- endurance;
- dexterity.

Quiet study:
You use the night for controlled form review, mobility, and pacing rather than another full daytime workout.

Difficult session:
Fatigue makes your movement sloppy, so you reduce the load and repeat the clean version instead of chasing intensity.

Successful insight:
A small change in stance makes the movement more efficient. You repeat it just enough to remember the difference.

Fatigue context:
The session stays deliberately light. The point is to learn from tired movement without turning tiredness into another contest.

Cheap-housing flavor:
With limited room, you rely on quiet mobility, balance, and bodyweight control rather than equipment-heavy training.

## Arts & Performance

Supported stats:
- creativity;
- charm;
- sociability;
- dexterity.

Quiet study:
You repeat a short phrase, step, rhythm, or sketch until one choice feels intentional instead of accidental.

Difficult session:
The piece feels flat no matter how many times you repeat it. You change only one element and listen for what finally shifts.

Successful insight:
A small timing change gives the work a clearer emotional direction. You note it before the idea disappears.

Fatigue context:
You keep the session compact, working on precision and intention rather than trying to force inspiration late at night.

Cheap-housing flavor:
You choose a quiet form of practice—notes, gestures, sketching, or silent rhythm—so the limited space becomes a constraint rather than a barrier.

## Body Confidence

Supported stats:
- sexuality;
- grooming;
- endurance.

Quiet study:
You move through a calm grooming or posture routine focused on deliberate self-care rather than display.

Difficult session:
You catch yourself performing for an imagined audience and restart with a choice that feels more like your own.

Successful insight:
A small change in posture or routine makes you feel more prepared without making you feel more artificial.

Fatigue context:
The session becomes slower and gentler, emphasizing comfort, boundaries, and steady movement instead of effort.

Cheap-housing flavor:
Privacy is limited, so the practice centers on small, discreet routines that still let you choose how you present yourself.

## Stat-family usage

The track-level variants above may be reused for any supported stat within that track.

When production text needs a stat-specific label:
- use the exact existing Night-choice identity;
- keep the prose presentation-only;
- do not add an extra stat effect in content.

## Housing guard

Housing-sensitive flavor may vary presentation only.

It must not:
- make street housing available for normal School Night study;
- change lodging costs;
- suppress the locked next-day Energy -1;
- add tuition;
- add a second Night-study progress increment.

## Reload guard

Any materialized Night-study presentation must preserve:
- chosen Night-study identity;
- selected presentation variant;
- already-committed Night mechanics;
- no reroll that changes mechanics;
- no duplicate effect on scene resolution.
