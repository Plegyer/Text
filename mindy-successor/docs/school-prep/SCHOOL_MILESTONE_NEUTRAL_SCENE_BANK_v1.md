# School Milestone-Neutral Scene Bank v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT. These scenes enrich School life without selecting milestone, exam, project, certificate, or public-result semantics.

No scene changes locked course mechanics, adds prerequisites, or implies completion of unresolved progression.

## Arrival / classroom moments

### Literacy & Numbers
Mara Fen is already halfway through arranging slates when you arrive. She gives you one without comment, then points to a badly copied notice on the wall. “Warm-up: find the sentence that would cost someone money if they misunderstood it.”

### Arcana
Ilyra Venn has covered one table with glass, chalk and labels. “Touch nothing yet.” She watches the room settle. “Today’s first exercise is patience. It is cheaper than replacing the apparatus.”

### Rhetoric & Social
Cassian Reed starts before everyone is seated. “Good. Half the room is distracted. Convince them they should care before I finish counting to ten.”

### Etiquette & Presentation
Eliane Marrow waits beside the doorway rather than the lectern. “Begin with the entrance,” she says. “People rarely get to redo that part.”

### Field Craft
Rowan Pike leaves a coil of rope in the wrong place on purpose. When someone moves it, he asks who noticed it first.

### Physical Training
Tovan Kest has the class check spacing before the first drill. “If you collide during the warm-up, that is not enthusiasm. That is poor planning.”

### Arts & Performance
Nessa Lark changes the room layout before anyone arrives. “If the space feels different, good. Now find out what that changes in you.”

### Body Confidence
Sera Vey begins with silence and a simple instruction: “Stand in a way you can maintain, not a way you think looks impressive.”

## Teacher feedback

Mara: “Your answer is fine. Your explanation is doing too much work. Simplify the path, not the thought.”

Ilyra: “You noticed the result. Now tell me what would have changed your mind.”

Cassian: “Better. You stopped performing confidence long enough to actually listen.”

Eliane: “That correction worked because it was small. Remember that before you redesign yourself for every room.”

Rowan: “You found the sign after I pointed at the area. Next time, find the area before I do.”

Tovan: “That looked easier because it was better organized, not because you tried less.”

Nessa: “Now I can tell what you meant. Keep that. Refine everything around it.”

Sera: “Good. You chose deliberately. That matters more than whether everyone else would choose the same thing.”

## Peer / context moments

- A classmate quietly asks to compare notes after Literacy & Numbers. The exchange stays social flavor unless a separate relationship scene is authored.
- Two Arcana students disagree about what they observed. Ilyra has each write the other person's interpretation before defending their own.
- Cassian pairs students with opposite rhetorical goals and tells them to switch positions halfway through.
- Eliane turns a minor etiquette mistake into a group exercise about helping someone recover gracefully.
- Rowan asks one student to hide an obvious clue and another to hide a subtle one, then compares which fooled the class.
- Tovan pairs students by pacing rather than strength for a cooperative drill.
- Nessa has the group repeat one short sequence while changing only intention.
- Sera asks the class to describe the difference between care, display, and conformity without judging any of them.

## Post-lesson reactions

These are presentation-only unless separately authored.

- “Mara marks one line in your notes and circles it twice: not because it is wrong, but because it is the clearest thing you wrote.”
- “Ilyra stays behind to relabel a sample and gives you a brief nod when you catch why.”
- “Cassian calls after you with a better version of your own joke and refuses to admit he had prepared it.”
- “Eliane returns one glove you forgot and says, ‘Preparation is easiest to notice when it is absent.’”
- “Rowan asks you to put the practice gear back exactly where the next group will expect it.”
- “Tovan reminds you that recovery is part of training and dismisses the class on time.”
- “Nessa changes one note in her rehearsal book after watching your experiment.”
- “Sera leaves the mirrors uncovered and the lights ordinary; the final exercise is to leave without checking yourself again.”

## Night-study flavor

Night study remains mechanically locked: zero tuition/current-day Energy, +2 selected supported stat, next-day Energy -1, one Night slot, Night counter only.

Flavor seeds:
- Literacy: copying a difficult passage until the structure becomes obvious.
- Arcana: reviewing diagrams by lamplight and writing down unanswered questions.
- Rhetoric: quietly rehearsing three ways to open the same conversation.
- Etiquette: practicing posture, introductions, and preparation in a quiet room.
- Field Craft: sorting notes, knots, maps, or route sketches before sleep.
- Physical Training: controlled mobility and form review rather than another full workout.
- Arts: repeating a short phrase, step, or sketch until one choice feels intentional.
- Body Confidence: a calm grooming or movement routine focused on deliberate self-care.

## Neutrality guard

Nothing in this bank:
- awards a certificate;
- completes an exam or project;
- crosses a milestone threshold;
- publishes a public result;
- grants a relationship stage;
- changes tuition, Energy, stats, Lust, or counters.

Those remain separate authored/system decisions.
