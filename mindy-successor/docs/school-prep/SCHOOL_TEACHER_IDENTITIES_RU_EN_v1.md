# School Teacher Identities RU+EN v1

Status: bilingual CONTENT_DRAFT for the eight existing drafted School teacher identities.
Rule: IDs, course/track assignment, gameplay role and visual identity are shared across locales. Russian is source authoring; English is the full second locale.

## `school.teacher.mara-fen`

Course:
- `school.course.literacy_and_numbers`
- `school.track.literacy_and_numbers`

RU name: **Мара Фен**

RU role: **городская писчая и преподавательница практической грамотности и счёта**

RU short bio:
Мара учит читать документы, считать деньги и объяснять сложное простыми словами. Она терпелива, точна и любит, когда ученик понимает, зачем нужен ответ, а не только как его получить.

EN name: **Mara Fen**

EN role: **civic scribe and practical literacy/numeracy instructor**

EN short bio:
Mara teaches how to read documents, handle practical arithmetic, and explain difficult ideas clearly. She is patient and exact, and cares more about understanding why an answer matters than merely producing it.

Shared gameplay role:
- Literacy & Numbers presenter;
- content/presentation only;
- no paid-lesson prerequisite.

Shared visual identity:
- ink-stained sleeves;
- slate board;
- ledgers;
- warm window light.

Translation notes:
- “писчая” is intentionally practical/civic rather than aristocratic “scholar.”

## Shared bilingual guardrails

Across all eight identities:
- canonical content ID is locale-neutral;
- course/track assignment is shared;
- visual identity is shared;
- no teacher is an ordinary paid-lesson prerequisite;
- no localization variant changes tuition, Energy, stats, Lust, progress, routing, or Save identity;
- relationship role remains optional and separately authored;
- management/accounting and Arcana/Forest public-result semantics remain OPEN.
