## Presentation rules

Every variant:
- is presentation-only;
- is keyed to the exact existing Night choice ID;
- may be selected deterministically by the normal event materialization path;
- must not apply an additional stat effect;
- must not apply another next-day Energy penalty;
- must not alter tuition or current-day Energy;
- must not increment either School counter itself.

Housing-sensitive presentation may be layered separately where current housing conditions are already supported, but housing flavor cannot change Night availability or mechanics.

## Reload rules

After the Night-study action has committed:
- the chosen presentation event may materialize once;
- the selected text variant persists across reload;
- resolving the presentation cannot repeat the Night transaction;
- the next-day Energy penalty remains owned by the Night action, not by content.
