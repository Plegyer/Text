## 1. Decision summary

### Recommended canonical model

**Model A — The Palace Concordance / Threshold Archive.**

The Palace is a real political-administrative institution built around an older and only partly understood **Threshold**: a real boundary phenomenon through which rare crossings between worlds can occur. The Palace does not create the world, simulate it, or invalidate ordinary life. Its deepest protected function is to keep a **Threshold Archive** and a **Concordance**: a cross-domain record that correlates work, routes, people, material evidence, institutional decisions, and anomalous arrivals/departures.

The ten ordinary careers matter because together they expose the heroine to the full practical cross-section of the successor world's life-sim systems. Permanent 10/10 Hard Work proves she has actually lived and mastered all ten domains across lives. Active 10/10 in the current life means she deliberately brings that whole synthesis into this run. Palace invitation is deterministic because a standing Palace protocol recognizes exactly that combination. It is not prophecy and not random selection.

The Prince is the current holder of the Palace's **Custodian office**: the final institutional gatekeeper for the Threshold Archive and for any attempt to use the return path. The Prince is a person occupying an office, not a deity, Palace avatar, metaphysical god, or compulsory romance figure.

The Palace's fifteen duties are a fixed **Concordance rotation**. They expose the heroine to service, logistics, records, coordination, formal interface, and evidence handling. The six stages are increasing levels of operational visibility and authority. By Outcome Ready, the heroine has seen enough of the Palace's real work, its network, and its contradictory records to make an informed choice about Service, Power, Truth, or Return.

The Prince confrontation is a deterministic authored proof/reconstruction challenge. “Defeating” the Prince means disproving his claim that only permanent custodial control can safely govern the heroine's origin trace, reconstructing a coherent return route from Palace evidence, and breaking his exclusive veto. It is not a physical defeat and does not kill, depose, romance, or magically dominate him.

Return Home is a real crossing back to the heroine's original world. The successor world remains real and continues to exist. Nothing is revealed as a dream or simulation, and prior lives are not erased.

### Product comparison

Model B supplies strong mystery but creates two unacceptable risks: it moves toward unexplained godhood and makes the Palace itself a quasi-agent whose rules can feel arbitrary. It also weakens the material connection to the life-sim because ordinary work becomes “resonance” rather than practical knowledge.

Model C is grounded and politically legible, but it narrows the final mystery into a conventional secret-monopoly story. The 12-stat True gate and the specific importance of all ten careers become harder to justify without turning into generic “you are very competent” requirements.

**Model A is the recommendation.** It keeps the Palace material, political, bureaucratic, strange, and connected to everything the heroine has actually done. It supports all four outcomes without requiring mutually exclusive metaphysics: Service preserves a useful institution, Court Power governs it, Inner Truth exposes its protected function, and Return Home uses the same truth to leave.

## 3. Canonical world authority — Model A

### 3.1 The Palace

Objective canon:

- The Palace is a real institution in the successor world.
- It performs ordinary visible administrative/service work and a protected second function.
- The protected function is stewardship of a real **Threshold** capable of rare crossings between worlds.
- The Palace maintains a **Threshold Archive** containing records, evidence, and provenance related to anomalous arrivals/departures.
- The Palace maintains a **Concordance**, meaning the institutional practice of correlating records from otherwise separate domains so no single department or profession owns the whole picture.
- The current successor world is not a dream, simulation, hallucination, or disposable copy.
- The heroine's ordinary lives and relationships are real regardless of which Palace ending is chosen.

### 3.2 Why the ten ordinary careers matter

Permanent ordinary Hard Work 10/10 is narratively interpreted as **lived breadth**. The heroine has not merely studied ten fields; she has repeatedly worked long enough to understand their real constraints, people, tradeoffs, and failure modes.

Current-run active 10/10 packages are narratively interpreted as **deliberate synthesis**. In this life she has chosen to carry all ten earned professional perspectives forward at once.

That exact combination is what the Palace protocol recognizes. No hidden eleventh profession, item, romance, Night scene, School course, Activity result, or relationship substitutes for it.

The invitation is deterministic because the Palace has a standing rule: a person satisfying both halves of D23 is entitled to be approached. Staff may not add another qualification test.

### 3.3 The Prince

Canonical role:

- The Prince is the current holder of the Palace's final **Custodian** office.
- The office has authority over access to the deepest Threshold Archive and the institutional decision to authorize a return attempt.
- The Prince is not a god, immortal abstraction, Palace avatar, or chosen-one oracle.
- The Prince does not need a personal romance with the heroine and no Palace ending assumes one.
- The Prince's central doctrine is **closed custody**: crossings should remain under narrow, centralized control because mistakes could strand people, misidentify origins, or destabilize existing lives.

The Prince wants the heroine to make an informed choice about the Palace, but he does not want Return Home to become a casual or purely personal entitlement. He prefers one of three controlled outcomes—continued service, institutional power exercised inside the Palace, or truth handled without opening the return route. If the heroine insists on Return Home, he requires her to demonstrate that she understands the system well enough to challenge his exclusive veto.

This motive can be sincere, self-protective, politically convenient, or all three. His sincerity is intentionally not resolved as objective canon.

### 3.4 Why fifteen duties and six stages exist in-world

The Palace uses a fixed fifteen-duty **Concordance rotation**. The duties are not mystical chores. They are normal institutional work arranged so that, over time, the worker sees the same problems from several operational angles.

The six stages mean:

1. **Entered Service** — she can perform Palace work but sees only local tasks.
2. **Working Rhythm** — she recognizes recurring procedures and shared records.
3. **Trusted Service** — she receives responsibilities that cross one local desk or team.
4. **Palace Network** — she sees how appointments, access, routing, and responsibility interact.
5. **Crossroads** — she is allowed to compare conflicting evidence and institutional explanations.
6. **Outcome Ready** — she has completed the full rotation and is permitted to make a final institutional choice.

The milestone numbers 2 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 15 remain purely the already-locked progression points. Narrative content explains them as clearance/responsibility steps; it does not add new mechanical checks.

### 3.5 What `palace.progression-complete` means

In-world, `palace.progression-complete` means:

**the heroine has completed the full Concordance rotation and has enough authenticated operational context to judge what the Palace is doing, what its power can change, and what evidence supports the Threshold claim.**

It does not mean:
- she has chosen an ending;
- she has defeated the Prince;
- she has opened Return Home;
- she has learned every historical secret;
- she has become Palace ruler;
- she has resolved every relationship.

### 3.6 Inner Truth

Inner Truth reveals the protected institutional truth:

- the Palace's deepest function is Threshold stewardship;
- the archive contains authenticated evidence of rare crossings that ordinary travel cannot explain;
- the heroine's own arrival belongs to that class of anomaly;
- Palace doctrine has deliberately distributed fragments of evidence across operational domains so no one ordinary role can reconstruct the whole system alone.

Inner Truth does **not** establish:
- why the heroine was originally displaced;
- who or what first created the Threshold;
- whether every historical anomaly was intentional;
- whether the Prince's family created the system or inherited it;
- that the current world is false.

Those questions remain intentionally ambiguous.

### 3.7 Court Power

Court Power controls **institutional levers**, not reality itself.

It can plausibly govern:
- appointments and responsibilities inside Palace service;
- access tiers and who may consult protected records;
- audit and review authority;
- internal routing and prioritization;
- formal introductions/recognition;
- conflict escalation and procedural exceptions;
- how Palace resources are coordinated among its service functions.

It does not make the heroine sovereign over the entire world, grant magical omnipotence, erase ordinary law, or automatically open the Threshold.

### 3.8 Service Life

Service Life chooses to preserve:

- reliable Palace operations;
- continuity of staff knowledge;
- professional boundaries;
- careful handoffs;
- institutional memory;
- the useful parts of a system that connects many ordinary domains.

It does not require believing every Palace claim, endorsing the Prince personally, or learning the final cause of the heroine's displacement.

### 3.9 Why all 12 progression stats >=91 matter

The True route requires a heroine who is not merely informed but **capable of acting as the stable point of reconstruction**.

The twelve progression stats collectively represent the broad physical, cognitive, magical/creative, social, presentational, and embodied capacities already used across the life-sim. At >=91 in all twelve, the heroine has no obvious single-domain weakness that the Palace can reasonably use to argue that the reconstructed origin trace depends on someone else's authority.

This is narrative justification for the already-locked numeric gate; it adds no further stat checks.

**Sexuality remains one of the twelve progression stats because it is already part of the progression model.** In this narrative it contributes to embodied self-knowledge and social/physical confidence only as an existing progression capacity. It never implies consent and never requires romance.

**Lust is excluded because it is a separate desire/intensity content axis, not a general competence or self-coherence stat.** High Lust is not impurity; low Lust is not virtue. Its value is morally and mechanically irrelevant to the True stat gate.

### 3.10 Return Home

“Home” means the heroine's **original world from before her arrival in the successor world**.

Return Home:
- is a real crossing;
- does not erase or rewrite the successor world;
- does not reveal prior lives as fake;
- does not retroactively invalidate relationships or work;
- does not merge the two worlds;
- does not require the heroine to hate the current world.

It is distinct from the three non-True endings because those endings choose a continuing relationship **inside** the Palace/world, while Return Home uses Palace truth to leave that relationship behind.

## 6. Prince confrontation contract

### 6.1 Chosen mechanical framing

v17 selects the previously researched **quest-backed latched event chain** shape from the v9 option space because it best matches the now-selected fiction.

Stable proposed identities:

- confrontation quest/state carrier: `palace.prince-confrontation`;
- explicit begin action: `palace.confront-prince`;
- durable success fact: `palace.prince-defeated`;
- Return Home ending remains: `ending.palace.return-home`.

No Activity entry, Activity item, romance, optional Night, relationship, School result, ordinary-career item, or legacy capability is required.

### 6.2 Availability

The explicit confrontation action is legal only when all are true:

- Palace access exists;
- Palace stage is Outcome Ready;
- `palace.progression-complete` exists;
- all 12 progression stats are >=91;
- Lust is ignored;
- `palace.prince-defeated` is absent;
- no incompatible pending scene/ending transaction is active under the normal runtime lifecycle.

No additional numeric threshold is authorized.

### 6.3 Four player-facing phases

#### Phase 1 — Audit the Record

Player-facing function:
reconstruct what the fifteen-duty rotation actually established.

Tests:
- understanding of the distinction between ordinary Palace work and the protected Threshold function;
- ability to identify which claims are supported by authenticated records.

Mechanical principle:
authored deterministic choices only. No RNG. No new stat check beyond the already-passed 12x91 gate.

Failure choice:
an unsupported conclusion ends the attempt without defeat.

#### Phase 2 — Name the Contradiction

Player-facing function:
the Prince presents the closed-custody case; the heroine must identify the point where institutional authority is being presented as if it were proof.

Tests:
- source/provenance reasoning;
- distinction between “the Palace controls access” and “the Palace alone knows truth.”

Mechanical principle:
choice correctness depends only on information available by Outcome Ready. Inner Truth ending completion is **not** required.

Failure choice:
accepting an unsupported absolute claim ends the attempt without defeat.

#### Phase 3 — Define the Boundary

Player-facing function:
the heroine must state what Return Home does and does not mean.

Correct conceptual boundary:
- leaving does not erase the current world;
- returning does not declare current relationships fake;
- refusing permanent custody is not the same as destroying the Palace;
- the heroine takes responsibility for choosing her origin without claiming ownership over everyone else's choices.

Tests:
whole-route comprehension and agency, not morality of Lust/romance.

Back-out:
the player may explicitly choose **Not yet**. This safely exits the confrontation.

#### Phase 4 — Reconstruct the Return Claim

Player-facing function:
assemble the final operational chain from authenticated origin trace -> verified boundary distinction -> requested return authorization.

Tests:
whether the player can put the already-revealed pieces into a coherent order.

Success:
- confrontation state becomes defeated/completed;
- `palace.prince-defeated` is written exactly once;
- no ending is auto-committed;
- Return Home may then become eligible under the existing ending flow.

### 6.4 Meaning of defeat

“Prince defeated” means:

- his claim to an **exclusive epistemic veto** has been disproved using the Palace's own authenticated record;
- the heroine has independently reconstructed a coherent return case;
- he can no longer truthfully claim that only his private judgment makes the route intelligible.

It does **not** mean:
- physical injury;
- death;
- magical domination;
- romance;
- forced abdication;
- automatic seizure of Court Power;
- destruction of the Palace.

### 6.5 Failure, back-out, retry

Exact v17 semantics:

- any authored failure ends the current confrontation attempt;
- explicit back-out ends the current attempt;
- no failure/back-out writes `palace.prince-defeated`;
- no failure/back-out commits an ending;
- no permanent “failed Prince” fact is written;
- confrontation state returns to the retryable ready state after the active scene chain has fully unwound;
- retry is allowed whenever the same readiness predicate is still true;
- no new Energy, money, item, Night, or calendar-time penalty is authorized by this packet;
- success is deterministic from the player's authored choices;
- reload resumes the exact active quest stage/pending latched scene rather than rerolling or skipping it.

Later implementation must not use permanent one-show scene semantics that make a legitimate retry impossible.

## 7. Ending epilogue authority

The following are epilogue contracts, not final prose. Existing ending IDs and D39/D24 rewards remain unchanged.

### 7.1 Service Life — `ending.palace.service-life`

**What the player chooses:**  
Remain inside Palace service and make continuity itself the chosen outcome.

**What changes in the current life:**  
The heroine is no longer treated as a probationary all-purpose worker. She becomes a recognized senior continuity figure whose value is that she understands how different Palace functions depend on one another.

**Relationship to the institution:**  
Affirmative but not submissive. She chooses the Palace as a place worth maintaining while retaining the right to question its claims and set professional boundaries.

**Explicitly not claimed:**
- she does not become ruler;
- she does not learn the original cause of her displacement;
- she does not endorse the Prince personally;
- she does not open or destroy the Threshold;
- romance is not implied.

**Epilogue beats:**
1. She accepts a durable Palace service role centered on handoffs, standards, and continuity.
2. She formalizes practices that prevent knowledge from depending on one patron, rival, or favored official.
3. Existing Palace relationships resolve according to their actual chosen states; none is rewritten into romance.
4. Her ten ordinary-career perspectives remain visibly useful in day-to-day Palace decisions.
5. The Palace continues as an imperfect but functioning institution, and she chooses to be part of its continuity.

Permanent reward remains exactly **Steady Practice** / `palace.service-life.steady-practice` / Specialist.

### 7.2 Court Power — `ending.palace.court-power`

**What the player chooses:**  
Accept formal responsibility for how Palace authority is allocated and exercised.

**What changes in the current life:**  
The heroine gains an institutional mandate over appointments, access review, coordination priorities, and formal escalation inside the Palace.

**Relationship to the institution:**  
She remains inside it but moves from service to governance. Her task is to make authority answerable to evidence and procedure rather than private patronage.

**Explicitly not claimed:**
- she does not become monarch of the whole world;
- she does not control reality or the Threshold by will;
- she does not automatically depose the Prince;
- she does not bypass Return Home requirements;
- romance is not implied.

**Epilogue beats:**
1. A formal mandate transfers real Palace decision authority to her.
2. She makes access and responsibility auditable instead of purely personal.
3. Patron, rival, and official-contact relationships respond according to prior choices, including opposition where appropriate.
4. Court politics remain real; the ending is power with constraints, not omnipotence.
5. The Palace continues under a more distributed and reviewable authority structure.

Permanent reward remains exactly **Formal Introduction** / `palace.court-power.formal-introduction` / Specialist.

### 7.3 Inner Truth — `ending.palace.inner-truth`

**What the player chooses:**  
Make the method of verifying Palace claims more important than holding office or preserving secrecy for its own sake.

**What changes in the current life:**  
The heroine secures access to the provenance structure of the Threshold Archive and establishes a practice of distinguishing direct observation, reported claims, contradictions, and unresolved uncertainty.

**Relationship to the institution:**  
Neither loyalist nor ruler by default. She may remain connected to the Palace as an investigator/archivist or step away from daily service; the ending's identity is epistemic, not positional.

**Explicitly not claimed:**
- the original cause of her displacement is solved;
- every Palace secret becomes public;
- the Prince is proven evil;
- Return Home opens automatically;
- the final mystery is reduced to one exposition dump.

**Epilogue beats:**
1. She secures the archive's provenance chain so claims can be checked against sources.
2. She records what is known, what is inferred, and what remains genuinely uncertain.
3. Palace staff can no longer rely as easily on “classified” as a substitute for evidence.
4. The heroine accepts that some historical questions remain unresolved without treating uncertainty as failure.
5. Her lasting legacy is a method for checking truth rather than a new throne or resource.

Permanent reward remains exactly **Source Method** / `palace.inner-truth.source-method` / automatic knowledge/content.

### 7.4 True Return Home — `ending.palace.return-home`

**What the player chooses:**  
After completing Palace progression, reaching all 12 stats >=91, and defeating the Prince's exclusive-veto doctrine, explicitly choose the origin world over every available continuing Palace future.

**What “home” means:**  
The real world the heroine came from before her arrival in the successor world.

**What changes in the current life:**  
The heroine uses the reconstructed origin trace and the Palace Threshold to cross back. This ending closes her current relationship to the Palace because she leaves its world rather than accepting a continuing Palace role.

**What she carries conceptually:**  
Memory of lived work, relationships, uncertainty, and the ability to distinguish authority from evidence. This is narrative meaning only; it grants no new mechanical reward beyond D24.

**What happens to Palace/Prince from her perspective:**  
The Palace remains behind as a real institution. The Prince's exclusive veto has been defeated, but he is not killed or automatically deposed. The heroine does not remain to control what political reforms follow.

**World state interpretation:**  
**Left behind, not rewritten.** The successor world continues. Return Home is neither simulation shutdown nor universal reset.

**Explicitly not claimed:**
- the current world was false;
- every person in it disappears;
- the heroine becomes a god;
- the heroine carries Palace items/resources across worlds;
- the Prince is destroyed;
- Lust has any moral bearing on the ending.

**Epilogue beats:**
1. The heroine explicitly declines the continuing Palace outcomes still available to her.
2. The verified origin trace is used to establish the crossing.
3. She crosses without the world collapsing, proving the Prince's absolute custody claim was too strong.
4. From the threshold, the Palace and its unresolved politics remain behind rather than being erased.
5. The final image/beat belongs to arrival in the original world and recognition that the life she left was real.

Permanent reward remains exactly `palace.true.cap-200` / Specialist, with D24 cap-only semantics.
