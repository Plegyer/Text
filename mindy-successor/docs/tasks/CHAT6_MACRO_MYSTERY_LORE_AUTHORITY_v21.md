## 1. Purpose

v21 closes the remaining 1.0 macro-mystery gap by defining exactly what may be known, suggested, inferred, or left unknown across the whole game before and after Palace.

It does **not** solve any D45 mystery that was intentionally left unresolved.

The governing principle is:

> The game may foreshadow the Palace mystery through records, routes, provenance, mismatches, repeated institutional habits, and first-hand observations. It may not foreshadow the answer by stating the Threshold, the cause of displacement, or Palace responsibility before those facts are earned.

---

## 2. Epistemic ladder

Use five lore levels.

### L0 — Lived discontinuity

Available before Palace.

The heroine/player may know:

- she had a life before the successor-world arrival;
- arrival into the successor world was abrupt enough to be experienced as a discontinuity;
- the successor world is the life she is actually living now;
- her own memory of “before” is meaningful to her.

L0 does **not** prove:

- that a known inter-world mechanism exists;
- that the Palace was involved;
- that the arrival was intentional;
- that the event can be reversed;
- that the current world is unreal.

Authoring rule:

Before Palace, use personal memory and practical uncertainty, not cosmic explanation.

---

### L1 — Local evidence habits

Available throughout ordinary careers, Night, School, Activities, relationships, poverty/Secret Debt, and other non-Palace systems.

The player may encounter:

- route reports that disagree;
- records whose timing does not match a convenient story;
- source/provenance questions;
- handoff errors;
- independently corroborated details;
- direct observation vs rumor;
- old boundaries, detours, missing information, or unexplained local inconsistencies;
- institutions that preserve imperfect records;
- people who confidently overstate what they know.

L1 may create the feeling that:

> this world contains information that becomes more reliable when different domains are compared.

L1 must **not** establish:

- Threshold;
- Threshold Archive;
- Palace Concordance;
- inter-world crossing as the explanation for a local anomaly;
- Palace authorship of any anomaly;
- a route home.

---

### L2 — Palace anomaly evidence

Begins at duty 12 / Crossroads.

The player may know:

- several authenticated records describe arrivals/departures ordinary travel cannot reconcile;
- separate institutional sources corroborate at least some anomalies;
- these are more than tavern rumor or one person's interpretation.

Still unknown:

- exact mechanism;
- the name/function of the Threshold;
- heroine-specific return route;
- original displacement cause;
- Prince's doctrine and motive.

Duty 12 remains the first point where the macro mystery moves from ordinary foreshadowing into authenticated anomalous evidence.

---

### L3 — Threshold authority

Begins at duty 15 / Outcome Ready.

The player may know as objective canon:

- the Palace has a protected Threshold-stewardship function;
- a real Threshold exists;
- rare inter-world crossings have occurred;
- the Threshold Archive contains authenticated crossing-related records;
- the Palace Concordance correlates records across otherwise separate domains;
- the heroine's own arrival is classified as a crossing anomaly;
- the successor world is real.

The player still does **not** know:

- why the heroine was originally displaced;
- who created or first discovered the Threshold;
- whether the current Prince's line created it or inherited it;
- whether all historical crossings were intentional;
- whether all historical crossings were voluntary;
- whether the Palace caused the heroine's arrival;
- whether the Prince's closed-custody doctrine is primarily sincere, self-serving, or both.

---

### L4 — Independent return proof

Established through the Prince confrontation.

The player may know:

- Palace records can be independently reconstructed into a coherent heroine-specific origin trace;
- institutional access control is not identical to exclusive truth;
- the Prince's exclusive epistemic veto can be defeated;
- a lawful return case exists without inventing the original displacement cause.

The confrontation proves:

- the evidence is sufficient to support a return claim;
- the Prince is not the only possible interpreter of that evidence.

It does **not** prove:

- that the Prince is evil;
- that the Palace is illegitimate in every respect;
- that centralized custody never reduces risk;
- that the Prince caused the heroine's arrival;
- that the Prince knows the original cause;
- that the Prince must be killed, deposed, or romantically converted.

---

### L5 — Return Home proof

Established only after explicit Return Home completion.

The player may know:

- the heroine's original world is reachable;
- the reconstructed return works;
- the crossing is real;
- the successor world remains real and intact;
- leaving does not erase prior lives, relationships, work, or history.

Return Home still does **not** prove:

- the original displacement cause;
- the identity of the first Threshold creator/discoverer;
- that every historical crossing was intentional;
- that every historical crossing can be reversed;
- that the Prince's motive was wholly selfish or wholly altruistic;
- what future political settlement the Palace adopts.

## 3. Pre-Palace knowledge ceiling

Before Palace, no optional system may give the player more than L1.

The strongest safe pre-Palace formulation is:

> “Several ordinary sources do not fit together cleanly, and careful comparison matters.”

The strongest unsafe pre-Palace formulation is:

> “These records are evidence of inter-world crossings.”

That conclusion belongs to Palace.

### Safe pre-Palace implications

Allowed:

- “The route report does not match the ledger.”
- “Two independent witnesses describe the same unusual timing.”
- “No one source proves the whole story.”
- “The old boundary appears in records from more than one profession.”
- “The source matters more than the confidence of the speaker.”
- “Something remains unexplained.”

Not allowed:

- “A portal caused this.”
- “The Palace is hiding crossings.”
- “This is part of the Threshold Archive.”
- “People have come from other worlds before.”
- “This is how you can return home.”

## 4. Existing content pattern audit

The existing production content already supplies a strong lore-compatible grammar.

Observed recurring patterns include:

### Careers

Existing cross-system career content repeatedly treats information as local, sourced, and non-universal.

Examples of existing content behavior:

- Guard uses Tavern receipts as external confirmation but leaves a missing source missing;
- Merchant records route/source facts without taking ownership of another system;
- Merchant preserves sample provenance;
- Assassin consumes route/rumor/cargo facts only as local context;
- Artist/Geisha accepts confirmed rumor or delivery context without converting it into a universal system;
- Prostitute cross-system content explicitly preserves source-system ownership.

This is fully compatible with D45.

Recommended lore use:

ordinary careers may foreshadow **the method of Concordance** without naming the Concordance.

---

### Tavern / information

Existing Tavern content already distinguishes:

- report;
- ledger evidence;
- direct observation;
- confirmed working information;
- rumor;
- source wording.

Recommended lore use:

Tavern is a safe place for reports that sound suggestive but remain epistemically limited.

A Tavern rumor may be eerie.

A Tavern rumor may not be retrospectively declared a hidden Threshold fact unless later Palace evidence independently corroborates the same ordinary detail.

---

### School

Existing School content explicitly teaches:

- evidence vs explanation;
- observed / tested / unknown;
- neutral questions;
- repeatable observation;
- reasoning that can be checked later.

Recommended lore use:

School may improve the heroine's vocabulary for uncertainty and provenance.

School must never teach the existence of the Threshold as required curriculum or become a Palace prerequisite.

---

### Activities

Existing Activity seams treat successful returns as:

- first-hand route evidence;
- source-confirmed observations;
- bounded facts about the route actually traveled.

Recommended lore use:

Activities may provide strange local observations, old boundaries, mapping discrepancies, or evidence that a route account is incomplete.

They may not provide:

- a required “portal clue”;
- a Threshold key;
- a return anchor;
- a required map fragment;
- a Prince-confrontation shortcut.

- цепочка ответственности

Do not imply:

## 6. Night foreshadowing authority

Night is allowed to carry emotional continuity, not new objective lore.

Safe Night functions:

- memory of the abrupt arrival;
- homesickness;
- uncertainty about what “home” now means;
- noticing that a daytime discrepancy still bothers the heroine;
- discussing competing interpretations with an existing relationship/contact;
- deciding to leave a question unresolved.

Unsafe Night functions:

- prophetic dream that reveals the Threshold;
- dream proving the successor world is unreal;
- Prince appearing supernaturally before Palace;
- a mandatory Palace vision;
- a romance-only lore answer;
- a Night choice that becomes a hidden Palace gate.

Secret Debt forced Night precedence remains absolute.

---

## 7. School foreshadowing authority

School may foreshadow the **epistemic method**, not the answer.

Safe School echoes:

- distinguish observation from explanation;
- identify source bias;
- compare independent accounts;
- preserve uncertainty explicitly;
- state what would falsify a claim;
- separate rhetorical confidence from evidence.

Unsafe School echoes:

- a textbook naming the Threshold before Palace;
- a course revealing Palace secret history;
- an Arcana lesson proving inter-world mechanics;
- a certificate or course count required for Palace;
- a teacher who definitively explains the heroine's origin.

School may make later Palace dialogue sound more precise.

School may never substitute for Palace evidence.

---

## 8. Activity foreshadowing authority

Activities may foreshadow through bounded first-hand experience.

Safe Activity motifs:

- an old boundary marker whose purpose is unclear;
- a mapped route that does not match current terrain;
- a return time inconsistent with an old account;
- an object/sample with unclear route provenance;
- two observations that agree on location but not explanation.

Unsafe Activity motifs:

- literal hidden Threshold entrance;
- required portal anchor;
- item that unlocks Palace;
- Activity result that skips duty 12 or 15;
- direct proof of the heroine's origin;
- a hidden “true route” to the original world.

An Activity result remains optional context even if Palace dialogue later references the heroine's familiarity with route verification.

---

## 9. Secret Debt foreshadowing authority

Secret Debt may foreshadow **institutional constraint**, not the Threshold.

Safe echoes:

- records and obligations can follow a person across housing/work changes;
- legal/institutional status and personal worth are not the same thing;
- constrained time/forced Night can affect what the heroine can practically do;
- later Palace dialogue may acknowledge that lawful Palace access remains valid despite unrelated economic pressure;
- Palace dialogue may recognize strain without removing or rewriting Debt consequences.

Secret Debt must never:

- contain Threshold evidence;
- grant Palace access;
- count as a Palace qualification;
- skip Palace duties;
- replace 12x91 readiness;
- suppress forced Night;
- alter repayment;
- make debt a metaphysical “price” of crossing.

No lore line should imply that Palace created, tracks, or intentionally uses Secret Debt.

---

## 10. Duty 12 authority

Duty 12 is the first authenticated macro-mystery turn.

Player may learn:

- multiple records from separate domains agree that at least some arrivals/departures cannot be reconciled with ordinary travel;
- the anomaly is real enough to survive cross-checking;
- records are incomplete.

Player may infer:

- a hidden mechanism may exist;
- Palace may know more than ordinary institutions;
- heroine's own arrival might belong to the same class.

At duty 12, content must not yet state as fact:

- Threshold;
- Threshold Archive;
- inter-world crossing;
- heroine-specific classification;
- Palace stewardship;
- Prince veto doctrine;
- route home.

Preferred vocabulary at duty 12:

EN:
- unresolved anomaly
- authenticated records
- separate sources
- route does not account for the event
- cross-check

RU:
- неразрешённая аномалия
- проверенные записи
- независимые источники
- маршрут не объясняет событие
- перекрёстная проверка

- Palace Concordance / Дворцовый Свод;

Still forbidden as objective fact:

- cause of the heroine's displacement;

- universal voluntariness of prior crossings;
- Prince's private motive.

## 12. Prince confrontation authority

The confrontation proves only what the evidence supports.

### Objective result

After success:

- the heroine has reconstructed a coherent Origin Trace / След происхождения;
- the Prince's exclusive epistemic veto no longer stands;
- `palace.prince-defeated` means the exclusive-veto doctrine was defeated.

### Supported but non-exclusive claim

The Palace/Prince may still reasonably claim:

- crossing mistakes can be dangerous;
- identity/origin mistakes matter;
- controlled access can reduce some classes of risk.

The confrontation does not require the player to prove all Palace safeguards unnecessary.

### Still interpretation

Still unresolved:

- whether Prince acts mostly from care;
- mostly from power;
- from institutional inertia;
- from all three.

### Explicit non-proof

Prince defeat does not prove:

- Prince is evil;
- Prince lied about every risk;
- Palace should be destroyed;
- Prince should be killed/deposed;
- heroine has sovereign authority;
- origin cause is known.

May remain unresolved:

---

### Inner Truth

Known by ending:

- prior anomalous crossings are real;

Explicitly still unknown:

- original displacement cause;
- first Threshold builder/discoverer;

### Return Home

Known by ending:

- a heroine-specific origin trace works;
- original world is reachable;

Still unknown after Return Home:

- whether every old crossing was intentional;

True ending closes the heroine's return question, not the cosmology.

## 15. Claim-writing rule for RU/EN authors

Every macro-mystery line should be classifiable as one of five forms.

### 1. Observation

EN:
“Two records give the same time.”

RU:
«Две записи указывают одно и то же время.»

Safe when directly supported.

### 2. Report

EN:
“A traveler says the road was empty.”

RU:
«Путник говорит, что дорога была пустой.»

Speaker claim only.

### 3. Inference

EN:
“That suggests the route account is incomplete.”

RU:
«Это позволяет предположить, что описание маршрута неполно.»

Use uncertainty language.

### 4. Institutional claim

EN:
“The Custodian says centralized control is necessary.”

RU:
«Хранитель утверждает, что централизованный контроль необходим.»

Do not silently upgrade to fact.

### 5. Objective canon

EN:
“The Threshold is real.”

RU:
«Порог реален.»

Use only after the appropriate reveal level.

If a line cannot be classified, it should not ship as macro-mystery canon until rewritten.

## 20. Product lock summary

v21 recommends Product lock the following macro-lore rules:

1. **Pre-Palace ceiling = L1 local evidence habits.**  
   Players may see suggestive mismatches, but not the inter-world answer.

2. **Duty 12 = authenticated anomaly, not Threshold reveal.**

3. **Duty 15 = first canonical naming of Palace Concordance / Threshold Archive / Threshold and heroine crossing classification.**

4. **Prince confrontation = proof of independent interpretation and return claim, not proof of original cause or Prince villainy.**

5. **Return Home = successful real crossing to the original world while the successor world remains real.**

6. **The cosmological unknowns remain unknown after every ending, including True Return Home:**
   - original displacement cause;
   - first Threshold creator/discoverer;
   - universal intent/voluntariness of historical crossings;
   - Prince's final private motive.

7. **Cross-system foreshadowing uses method, not answers:**
   records, routes, sources, handoffs, cross-checking, direct observation, bounded inference, and honest unknowns.

8. **No optional echo is ever a Palace gate.**

This closes the 1.0 macro-mystery authoring authority without reopening D45.
