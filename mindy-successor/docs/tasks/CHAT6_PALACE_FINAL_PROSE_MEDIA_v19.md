- custodian of the protected Threshold Archive.

The Prince remains:

- an office-holder;
- the Palace Custodian;
- the final institutional gatekeeper over protected records and return authorization.

He is not presented as:

- a god;
- an avatar;

- an immortal abstraction;
- a combat boss;
- a mandatory romance.

His defeat remains epistemic/procedural, not physical.
