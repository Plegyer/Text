## Per-career authoring notes

### Lumber

`lumber.foreman` is the only relationship identity in current root production definitions. Its current professional route is real shipped content; this matrix does not rename it or add stages. Additional Foreman trust, close, rival, School, Hunter, Old Boundary, or ending semantics remain Product-open.

### Satyr Maid

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A senior service-role contact would let relationship content grow naturally from ordinary shifts without requiring a generic daytime date action. The proposed Night continuation should be an after-closing conversation or public walk, not an automatic romance route.

### Undead Farm

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A practical farm specialist creates a contrasting relationship shape: competence, odd farm logistics, samples/tools, and shared problem-solving. This draft deliberately does not require a Night continuation; relationship depth can come from work and external-item/Activity reactions.

### Tavern

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A recurring information-oriented contact could eventually be shared with another career, but no sharing is selected here. Any rumor/world-fact identity must be authored and published explicitly before another system consumes it.

### Hunter

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A trail partner can support items, preparation, and Activity feedback. This role must not silently define the unresolved Hunter trail, Lumber Forest, or Old Boundary semantics.

### Guard

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A patrol partner supports a peer/rival structure rather than a romance-only pipeline. Evidence, case, Tavern, or School hooks are proposals until a public identity exists.

### Merchant

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A supplier/client relationship can express trust as reliable trade behavior rather than affection points. Any accounting/management School link must wait for a Product-approved public School result.

### Artist / Geisha

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A performance peer or instructor can connect School and career content while keeping ordinary lessons independently available. This draft does not select Palace access, court roles, or relationship endings.

### Assassin

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** A handler or rival specialist provides a deliberately less domestic relationship archetype. No School dependency is inferred because current design authority does not require one.

### Prostitute

**CONTENT_DRAFT / PRODUCT_NOT_LOCKED.** The ordinary career relationship must remain voluntary and boundary-aware. It cannot borrow forced Night logic, debt obligation, or progression from Secret Debt/Slave content.

## Cross-roster principles

The final roster should avoid ten copies of the same romance pipeline. The proposed spread intentionally includes:

- professional mentors;
- peers and rivals;
- practical specialists;
- information contacts;
- business patron/client roles;
- field partners;
- boundary-aware ordinary-career contacts.

A future Product decision may make one NPC span multiple careers. Until that decision exists, this matrix does not assign any shared identity.

## Night coverage interpretation

“Yes” means **possible authored continuation**, not a requirement that the NPC gets romance or an ending. “No” means this draft intentionally demonstrates that a meaningful relationship can stay in daytime/reactive career content and external hooks without a Night route.

Any approved Night continuation must:

- use the voluntary Night slot;
- cost 0 daytime Energy;
- avoid a universal next-day Energy penalty;
- preserve explicit player choice;
- respect housing/privacy conditions where authored.

## Cross-hook discipline

The “possible cross-hook” column names only legal *shapes*. It does not invent production facts. Before implementation, every cross-system path needs either:

- an already-shipped exact identity, or
- a separately approved public fact/result/item/capability/Activity result.

Private progress from another subsystem must not be read directly.

## Presentation discipline

Every future relationship event should own one locale-neutral semantic `presentationId`, RU+EN text/alt coverage, static/text fallback, and—where repetition justifies it—a future 3–5 GIF candidate opportunity beneath the same gameplay identity.

A proposed visual need is not a shipped asset. Placeholder GIF paths must remain staging evidence only.
