## 4. Interpretation

The intended meta curve works.

One strong ending visibly changes the next run.

Two strong endings:
- make the mid-tier profession layer almost immediately reachable;
- bring late jobs forward by roughly one to two seasons.

Automatic Hard Work bonuses stack on top of this.

Therefore 2 pre-Palace strong Legacy slots are enough to produce substantial easing.

## 5. Perk identity

+1 Energy is the strongest broad action-economy reward.
No Rent is the strongest survival/economy reward.
School discount is a directed-stat reward.
Starting money is strongest at the opening and naturally loses relative value later.

This differentiation is desirable: endings support different future builds.

## 6. Balance warning

Do not nerf these simply because they are powerful.

Power is the point of meta progression.

Control instead:
- ending difficulty;
- number of active major perks;
- conflicts only where mechanically necessary.

## 7. Loadout conclusion

Current candidate remains:
- automatic Hard Work rewards;
- 2 strong Legacy slots before Palace;
- 3 after Palace.

This produces a large but controllable power curve.
