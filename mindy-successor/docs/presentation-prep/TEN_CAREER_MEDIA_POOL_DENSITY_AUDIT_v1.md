## Reuse-density guidance

### Cross-season reuse

A seasonal pool does not need 3–5 completely unique motions.

A practical pool can contain:
- 1–2 career-level reusable motions;
- 1–3 season/context-specific variants.

### Cross-career reuse

Allowed when the visual action is genuinely generic:
- indoor service;
- outdoor work;
- city-duty movement;
- guest-facing preparation;
- logistics.

Cross-career reuse must remain media-only.

### Stable result/default

The result slot may:
- reuse completed-work candidates from seasonal shift pools;
- add one dedicated result candidate;
- remain static-only until polish is approved.

Dedicated result GIFs are not required for correctness.

## Additional pools that remain content-first

The 50 base surfaces above are shipped.

Do not invent extra presentation IDs for:
- Hard Work endings not shipped;
- named relationship scenes;
- milestone events;
- Night events;
- Palace hooks;
- Activity continuations;
- contract/target identities;
- client identities.

Those receive pools only after their gameplay/content identities are authored.
