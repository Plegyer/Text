# Secret Debt Hidden Career Work Bank v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT scene bank for repeatable daytime work in the hidden Secret Debt career.

This file authors work situations and flavor only. It does not define wages, stat gains, Energy cost, stage thresholds, debt reduction, outcome eligibility, or Meta rewards.

## Fixed mechanical boundary

The directed hidden-career structure is:
- available only while the authoritative Secret Debt route is bound/active;
- separate from the 10 ordinary professions;
- repeatable daytime work exists;
- forced Night remains a separate NightFlow obligation;
- ordinary career progress is preserved while ordinary jobs are blocked;
- Secret Debt work does not grant ordinary Hard Work credit;
- exact progression numbers remain OPEN.

## Work family A — sorting and carrying

Tone:
routine, physical, impersonal.

Draft scene:

Crates arrive faster than anyone bothers to label them.

The instructions are simple:
- move what is marked;
- stack what is not;
- ask only when something is impossible to guess.

Nothing about the job is dramatic.

That is part of the pressure.

### Compliance flavor

> You settle into the rhythm and finish the stack without giving anyone a reason to stop you.

### Resistance flavor

> You do exactly what was asked and nothing that was merely implied.

No hidden mechanical bonus/penalty is implied by tone choice.

### Identity-neutral reaction hook

A supervisor-like role may note:
- reliable;
- difficult;
- observant;
- quiet.

Canonical relationship identity remains OPEN.

## Work family B — cleaning after everyone leaves

Tone:
low-status, repetitive, late-day physical work.

Draft scene:

The room looks different once the paying customers, workers, or owners are gone.

What remains is:
- spilled drink;
- mud;
- ash;
- broken packing straw;
- things nobody noticed while the room was busy.

The heroine is expected to make the mess disappear before morning.

### Compliance flavor

> You finish methodically, corner by corner.

### Resistance flavor

> You clean what the job requires and leave every personal errand untouched.

### World reaction hook

A later public fact may reflect that the heroine knows:
- back corridors;
- storage layout;
- closing routines.

Do not publish such facts unless a real downstream consumer exists.

## Work family C — delivery under supervision

Tone:
movement with constrained autonomy.

Draft scene:

A sealed package has to cross town.

The route would be ordinary if someone were not making sure you took it.

You are told where to go.

You are told when to return.

### Compliance flavor

> You take the shortest route and bring back the receipt.

### Resistance flavor

> You remember every turn and every face that watches you.

Potential future hooks:
- world familiarity;
- ally/contact opportunity;
- takeover reconnaissance.

Those remain content hooks only until explicitly authored.

## Work family D — inventory count

Tone:
tedious work with information value.

Draft scene:

Shelves, boxes, ledgers.

The work is to count what belongs to someone else and make sure the numbers match.

### Compliance flavor

> You copy the totals exactly.

### Resistance flavor

> You copy the totals exactly and remember which entries make no sense.

Potential future route hook:
- knowledge of stock, money flow, keys, or records.

Do not infer an automatic takeover requirement.

## Work family E — service under observation

Tone:
social pressure rather than physical pressure.

Draft scene:

The task itself is simple.

The difficult part is being watched while doing it.

Every pause is noticed.

Every question takes too long.

### Compliance flavor

> You keep your expression neutral and finish cleanly.

### Resistance flavor

> You give them nothing except the work they can prove they asked for.

Potential future relationship hook:
- respect;
- suspicion;
- rivalry.

No canonical NPC is selected.

## Work family F — unpleasant maintenance

Tone:
dirty, physically tiring, non-terminal.

Draft scene:

Something has clogged, leaked, jammed, or rotted.

The job lands with you because nobody important wants it.

### Compliance flavor

> You solve the immediate problem and move on.

### Resistance flavor

> You make sure the repair works, but you refuse every suggestion that this is somehow a favor.

No stat or condition effect is fixed here.

## Work family G — records after hours

Tone:
mental strain, coercive bureaucracy.

Draft scene:

A ledger is placed in front of you with instructions that assume you already know the notation.

The columns do not care whether the day was long.

### Compliance flavor

> You work line by line until the pages balance.

### Resistance flavor

> You work carefully enough to notice what the numbers reveal.

Possible future public result:

- debt-record knowledge.

Exact fact IDs remain OPEN.

## Work family H — controlled errand

Tone:
small task that demonstrates loss of ordinary professional freedom.

Draft scene:

It would take anyone else a few minutes.

For you, it comes with a reminder:

> "This first."

The task is not dangerous.

The lack of choice is the content.

### Compliance flavor

> You finish it quickly and reclaim what remains of the day.

### Resistance flavor

> You finish it slowly enough to make the point, but not slowly enough to hand them an excuse.

Mechanics must not infer time cost beyond the configured action cost.

## Work family I — heavy shift

Tone:
escalating physical pressure.

Draft scene:

Today's work is heavier than the earlier assignments.

Nobody announces that the standard has changed.

They simply put more in front of you.

### Compliance flavor

> You pace yourself and finish.

### Resistance flavor

> You refuse to make exhaustion look like gratitude.

No Strength/Endurance gain is fixed here.

## Work family J — trusted access

Tone:
late-career escalation; responsibility creates opportunity.

Draft scene:

For the first time, you are sent somewhere without someone standing over your shoulder.

It is not freedom.

It is trust of a narrow, useful kind.

### Compliance flavor

> You return exactly as expected.

### Resistance flavor

> You return exactly as expected, carrying more knowledge than you left with.

Potential future takeover hook:

- route knowledge;
- keys;
- schedules.

No one of these is canonically required.

## Escalating pressure ladder

This is narrative ordering guidance only.

### Early work

Use:
- sorting/carrying;
- cleaning;
- basic errands;
- maintenance.

Theme:
the hidden career is restrictive but still resembles ordinary low-status labor.

### Middle work

Use:
- supervised deliveries;
- service under observation;
- records/inventory;
- more demanding shifts.

Theme:
the heroine learns the system while the system expects more from her.

### Late work

Use:
- trusted access;
- sensitive records;
- unsupervised errands;
- higher-responsibility work.

Theme:
dependency and competence grow at the same time.

Do not convert this ladder into numeric thresholds without Product/content authority.
