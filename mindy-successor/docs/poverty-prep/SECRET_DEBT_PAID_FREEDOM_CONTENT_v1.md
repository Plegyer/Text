# Secret Debt Paid Freedom Content v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT narrative shell for the Secret Debt outcome family `paid_freedom`.

This file does not define the exact eligibility threshold, exact debt amount, payment formula, ending condition, or Meta reward.

## Outcome identity

`paid_freedom` is the economic-resolution ending family.

Its fixed narrative meaning:
- the heroine resolves the dependency through payment/economic settlement;
- the Secret Debt professional lock ends;
- the outcome is distinct from endurance/submission and takeover;
- no ordinary Hard Work credit is granted;
- any Meta reward is applied only after generic ending completion and remains OPEN.

## Approach scene family

### Approach A — count it twice

The heroine counts the money once.

Then again.

Not because the amount might change, but because this is the first time the total can mean something other than survival.

Draft internal line:

> Enough to stop owing tomorrow to yesterday.

The production UI may show authoritative configured values separately. This prose must not embed a guessed amount.

### Approach B — ask for the account

The heroine requests a final accounting.

Lender/employer role:

> "You want the number?"

Heroine:

> "I want the end."

The response should expose only authoritative configured state when production mechanics are known.

### Approach C — arrive prepared

The heroine comes to the meeting with the means to settle.

No pleading.

No negotiation assumed.

Draft:

> This time, you are the one who puts something on the table first.
