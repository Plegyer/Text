# Secret Debt Takeover Content v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT narrative shell for the Secret Debt outcome family `takeover`.

This file does not define exact progression requirements, exact stat/build checks, the selected external dependency, exact item/fact IDs, or Meta rewards.

## Outcome identity

`takeover` is the multi-system power solution.

Its fixed meaning:
- the heroine does more than endure or repay;
- she uses accumulated hidden-career knowledge plus at least one external dependency/source of leverage;
- the old controlling relationship is ended or inverted;
- the heroine takes meaningful control;
- the route requires more than shifts alone;
- no ordinary Hard Work credit is granted;
- exact Meta reward remains OPEN.

## Progression context

The narrative should make takeover feel earned through accumulated access and knowledge.

Useful prior content families:
- records;
- inventory;
- trusted deliveries;
- hidden routes;
- private ledgers;
- staff routines;
- keys/access;
- relationship trust;
- public/School/Activity/item facts chosen later.

No single family is mandatory until Product defines exact route requirements.

## Approach scene family

### Approach A — the system is visible now

Early in the hidden career, the institution felt like a wall.

Later, the heroine can see:
- who actually gives orders;
- who only repeats them;
- where money moves;
- which routines depend on one person;
- which doors are locked because everyone assumes they will stay locked.

Draft internal line:

> The trap stopped looking like a trap when you learned where all its hinges were.

### Approach B — leverage exists

The heroine holds one piece of external leverage.

The exact source is OPEN.

Possible categories:
- item;
- ally/relationship result;
- School public result;
- Activity result;
- world fact;
- another typed/public system result.

Draft:

> For the first time, something important exists outside their control.

### Approach C — route choice

The heroine decides not to leave quietly.

Possible commitment labels:
- **Take control**
- **Turn the account around**
- **Use what you know**
- **End this on your terms**

These are CONTENT_DRAFT.

### Preparation A — verify the weakness

The heroine checks that the leverage is real before acting.

Possible scene forms:

- retrieve an item;
- verify a public fact;

The actual dependency must come from authoritative system state.

### Preparation B — choose who knows

### Preparation C — prepare the workplace

The heroine arranges:

No mechanical effect is implied unless authored.

## Resolution scene

### Core confrontation

The controlling figure expects another ordinary exchange.

The heroine changes the terms.

Draft:

> "We're not discussing what I owe."
>
> "Then what are we discussing?"
>
> "What happens when everyone learns how this place actually works."

This line should be adapted to the chosen leverage type.

### Power inversion variant

If leverage is practical rather than public:

> "You need the routes, the records, and the people who know how to keep this running."
>
> "I have all three."

Do not use this exact line unless production state supports its claims.

### Quiet takeover variant

No dramatic confrontation is required.

The old owner/controller discovers:
- access has shifted;
- records no longer answer only to them;
- staff follows the heroine's plan;
- the decisive external dependency has already been triggered.

Draft:

> By the time they realize the decision has been made, the decision is old news.

## What takeover must NOT become

Do not author it as:
- simple 50-shift equivalent;
- automatic reward for high hidden-career seniority;
- simple debt payment;
- a single stat check with no external dependency;
- generic violence-only route;
- an ordinary Hard Work ending.

The route identity is multi-system leverage plus hidden-career progression.
