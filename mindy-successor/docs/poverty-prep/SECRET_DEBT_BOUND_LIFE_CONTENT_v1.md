# Secret Debt Bound Life Content v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT narrative shell for the Secret Debt outcome family `bound_life`.

This file does not define exact progression thresholds, exact stage requirements, commitment counters, debt values, or Meta rewards.

## Outcome identity

`bound_life` is the hidden-career endurance/fate outcome.

Its fixed meaning:
- the heroine remains inside the Secret Debt dependency as a chosen terminal life/career outcome;
- it is not a generic failure screen;
- it requires substantial hidden-career progression before it may become eligible;
- commitment is explicit;
- while the active run remains bound before terminal completion, ordinary jobs remain blocked;
- Secret Debt still grants zero ordinary Hard Work credit;
- exact Meta reward remains OPEN.

## Approach scene family

### Approach A — the work has become familiar

The heroine finishes a task without asking where anything is kept.

Nobody gives directions.

Nobody checks the result.

The routine that once felt temporary now has habits, shortcuts, and expectations.

Draft internal line:

> I know how this place works because I have spent too long learning it.

This line reflects substantial progression narratively but does not encode a threshold.

### Approach B — someone else asks her

A newer worker asks the heroine:
- where something belongs;
- who to avoid;
- what a signal means;
- how to survive a difficult shift.

The scene shows that she is no longer at the beginning of the hidden career.

No mentor relationship is canonically created.

### Approach C — offered permanence

A supervisor/employer-like role may say:

> "You could stop treating this like something you're leaving."

The offer is not mechanically valid until the authoritative ending eligibility exists.

## Commitment scene

The outcome must feel chosen, even inside constrained circumstances.

Possible draft setup:

The heroine is given an opportunity to stop arguing about whether this life is temporary.

Not freedom.

Not takeover.

A decision to remain.

### Commitment label candidates

CONTENT_DRAFT only:
- **Stay**
- **Make this the life you know**
- **Stop planning an exit**
- **Accept the place you have made here**

Production should select one clear explicit commitment action.

### Commitment dialogue A

Heroine:

> "I'm done pretending I'm leaving next week."

Counterparty:

> "Then act like you belong here."

### Commitment dialogue B

Heroine:

> "I know the work. I know the rules. I know what this costs."

> "And?"

> "I'm staying."

### Commitment dialogue C — quiet

No formal speech.

The heroine simply puts away the plan, note, key, savings envelope, or other symbol of leaving.

The exact prop is CONTENT_DRAFT.

## Why this is not a failure ending

Authoring must avoid framing such as:
- "you lost";
- "you failed to escape";
- "game over because debt";
- "no choices remain."

Instead, the scene should establish:
- competence;
- accumulated history;
- conscious commitment;
- a life shaped by constraint but still recognized as an ending family.

## Immediate aftermath

### Version A — morning routine

The next morning is described not as another temporary day, but as a routine the heroine now recognizes as hers.

> Nobody needs to tell you where to begin.

### Version B — keys or responsibility

A role may grant the heroine:
- responsibility;
- trusted access;
- scheduling duty;
- authority over a narrow task.

This is narrative status only unless production explicitly grants a fact/capability.

### Version C — social recognition

Another worker says:

> "So you're really staying."

Heroine response variants:
- "Looks like it."
- "I made the choice."
- silence.

## Ending commitment boundary

Production conversion should keep:
`eligible -> explicit commit -> completeEnding -> RunResult`

Eligibility is not commitment.

A heroine who qualifies for `bound_life` must still be able to keep playing/seek another route until she explicitly commits, unless Product later chooses otherwise.

## Palace firewall

`bound_life` may be a Hard-Work analogue inside the hidden career's narrative family, but it is NOT:
- an ordinary `hardWork:<career>` reward;
- an eleventh ordinary career;
- Palace prerequisite credit.

It contributes zero toward the 10 ordinary Hard Work requirement.
