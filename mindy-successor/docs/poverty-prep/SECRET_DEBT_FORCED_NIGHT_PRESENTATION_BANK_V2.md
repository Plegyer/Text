# Secret Debt Forced-Night Presentation Bank v2

Status: **AUTHORING_ONLY / NOT PRODUCTION GAMEPLAY**.

This bank expands RU+EN presentation for the already-established Secret Debt forced-Night concept.

It does not choose:
- interception policy;
- frequency;
- trigger probability;
- which ordinary Night choices are replaced or deferred;
- lodging interaction;
- Energy or stat penalties;
- next-day modifiers;
- debt progression;
- hidden-work progression;
- outcome thresholds;
- Meta rewards.

Russian is the source authoring language; English is the full second locale.

## Structural boundary

The concept is fixed only at a high level:
- while Secret Debt is in its future bound state, some Nights may be intercepted by an obligation;
- the forced-Night scene is separate from ordinary voluntary Night content;
- ordinary-career progress remains preserved while bound;
- Secret Debt remains outside Palace 10/10.

The exact moment, frequency, cost, and mechanical consequence of any forced Night remain Product-open.

# Interception policy remains Product-open

This bank deliberately does **not** answer:
- whether forced Night intercepts before lodging;
- whether it intercepts after lodging;
- whether lodging cost is still paid;
- whether voluntary Night choice is replaced, deferred, or skipped;
- whether School Night Study remains available;
- whether Foreman Night is blocked;
- whether the intercept is random, scheduled, state-driven, or content-authored;
- how often it can happen;
- whether multiple forced Nights can occur consecutively.

- does not create an ordinary career shift;
- does not award ordinary Hard Work;
- does not count toward Palace 10/10;
- must preserve ordinary-career progress while the future bound state blocks ordinary-career access.
