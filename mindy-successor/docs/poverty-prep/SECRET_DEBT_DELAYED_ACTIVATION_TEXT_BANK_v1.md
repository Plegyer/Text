# Secret Debt Delayed Activation Text Bank v1

Status: CONTENT_DRAFT narrative bank for the interval between accepted lender offer and authoritative Secret Debt activation.

This file defines no activation delay, debt amount, payment schedule, job lock, forced-Night penalty, or runtime representation.

## Fixed narrative arc

The directed structure is:
1. the offer was accepted;
2. immediate relief is real;
3. nothing binds the heroine immediately;
4. later signs make the earlier exchange feel less casual;
5. activation notice arrives at the authoritative delayed boundary;
6. the actual bound-state mechanics begin only when runtime/content authority transitions the route.

## Phase A — temporary relief

### Variant A1 — a room tonight

The immediate benefit solves a real problem.

For one night, there is no need to count the same coins twice.

No messenger appears.

No demand follows.

The silence makes the exchange easy to reinterpret as less dangerous than it felt.

Draft internal tone:

> Maybe it really was only money.

This line is heroine perspective, not system truth.

### Variant A2 — ordinary purchases feel possible again

A small purchase no longer threatens tomorrow's lodging.

That change is almost enough to make the lender disappear from memory.

The heroine notices the relief first:
- food without immediate arithmetic;
- a paid room if affordable under actual game state;
- room to choose work/School/other actions according to existing systems.

Do not author guaranteed purchases the runtime cannot actually support.

### Variant A3 — no immediate consequence

Another day passes with no visible demand.

If the player expected a trap, the lack of one is its own pressure.

Draft prose:

> Nothing happened.
>
> By evening, that almost felt reassuring.

No exact number of days should appear.

## Phase B — ambiguous reminders

These scenes may be authored as one or more non-binding reminders before activation.

They must not:
- change debt mechanically unless explicitly configured later;
- block ordinary careers before authoritative activation;
- trigger forced Night early;
- reveal exact later debt values unless Product chooses to.

### Variant B1 — familiar handwriting

A folded note appears where the heroine will find it.

It contains no demand.

Only:

> "I haven't forgotten."

No signature is required in the draft.

### Variant B2 — a person who does not stop

Across a crowded street, the heroine briefly sees someone who may be connected to the lender.

They do not approach.

They do not follow.

The uncertainty is the content.

Possible line:

> The figure looks away first.

No hidden tracking mechanic is implied.

### Variant B3 — account language

A small card arrives with a single phrase:

> "Your account remains open."

This is deliberately vague.

It must not be interpreted as:
- a numeric ledger UI;
- a growing interest formula;
- an exact deadline.

### Variant B4 — reminder at the wrong moment

The heroine finds a token, note, or mark after an otherwise normal day.

The scene should feel intrusive without changing the action that just completed.

Possible text:

> The day was yours right up until the reminder made you think about who else believed they had a claim on it.

No ownership/legal status is mechanically asserted by this line.

### Variant B5 — silence becomes suspicious

No reminder arrives.

The heroine notices the absence.

> You catch yourself wondering why nobody has come yet.

This can be used as variation without implying the activation is delayed again.

## Phase C — pre-activation warning

Optional CONTENT_DRAFT variants for a stronger sign shortly before activation.

Whether such a warning exists at all is content-owned.

### Variant C1 — "soon"

A message contains one word that matters:

> "Soon."

Nothing else.

No number.

No time of day.

No exact payment request.

### Variant C2 — request to be available

> "Be available when called."

This line creates anticipation only.

It must not itself:
- block jobs;
- block School;
- block Activity;
- consume Night.

### Variant C3 — a meeting request

A location may be supplied only after world/content ownership approves one.

Draft:

> "We need to discuss the rest."

No exact debt amount appears.

## Phase D — activation notice

This is the scene family that accompanies the actual authoritative state transition.

The runtime/state transition must happen through the accepted implementation owner. Text alone does not create bound state.

### Variant D1 — the amount is no longer the point

The lender or intermediary returns.

> "The advance is no longer the part that matters."

The heroine asks what is owed.

Draft response:

> "Enough that we are done pretending this was casual."

This avoids inventing a number.

If Product later wants the exact configured debt displayed, the UI may present it from authoritative state separately.

### Variant D2 — work instead of payment

The notice makes the change explicit without authoring exact economics:

> "You can settle this in money if you have it."
>
> "If you don't, there is work."

Whether immediate payoff is actually available at activation remains tied to approved mechanics.

Do not imply a payoff threshold beyond authoritative configuration.

### Variant D3 — professional lock reveal

Draft narrative:

> "Until this is settled, your work belongs here."

This line should only be used if the production scene occurs at the same transaction boundary that actually applies the bound-state ordinary-career lock.

Never show it before the lock is authoritative.

### Variant D4 — no theatrical threat

The activation may remain quiet:

> "You accepted help."
>
> "Now the account changes."

The power of the scene can come from the mechanical consequence rather than explicit violence.

### Variant D5 — heroine pushes back

Heroine:

> "That wasn't the agreement."

Response:

> "It was the part you didn't ask to read."

This is CONTENT_DRAFT flavor only.

Do not treat it as legal canon unless Product explicitly authors contractual terms.

## Save/reload authoring requirements

When converted to production:
- a reminder already selected must not reroll on reload;
- reminder text must not increment the activation clock by itself;
- activation notice must not replay after authoritative transition to bound;
- immediate lender grant must not replay at activation;
- reload at pending state preserves the same authoritative activation boundary;
- reload at bound state remains bound.
